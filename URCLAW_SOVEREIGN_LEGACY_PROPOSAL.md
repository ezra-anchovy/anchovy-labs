# Product Concept: UrClaw Sovereign Legacy (v1.0)
**Goal:** A 'Digital Inheritance' protocol that ensures your assets, private keys, and digital sanctuary pass to your heirs without centralized cloud dependency.

## 1. The Problem
Current digital estate planning (LastPass, Apple Legacy, Google Inactive Manager) relies on centralized gatekeepers. If the gatekeeper denies access or suffers a breach, the legacy is lost. 

## 2. The Sovereign Solution
- **The Dead-Man Switch:** A local script running on the UrClaw Appliance that triggers a series of encrypted releases if the user fails to 'Check In' (Physical or Digital) for a set period.
- **Encrypted Shard Distribution:** Uses Shamir's Secret Sharing to split the Sanctuary's master key into shards distributed to trusted heirs/legal counsel. No single person has the key until the Switch is triggered.
- **Personal AI Legacy Agent:** A fine-tuned Llama-3.2-3B model trained on your 'Sovereignty Philosophy' that guides heirs through the technical process of reclaiming the sanctuary.

## 3. Product Package
- **UrClaw Legacy Add-on:** $1,499 one-time configuration fee.
- **Sovereign Trust Management:** $99/mo (includes secure hardware backups and quarterly legacy testing).
