# PRD: SimplifiedClaw Legal Swarm (v1.0)
**Concept:** Institutional-grade agent swarm for boutique law firms (5-50 attorneys).
**Positioning:** "Rigor over Hype. Auditability as an Architectural Requirement."

## 1. Problem Statement
Boutique law firms are being squeezed by "Big Law" AI adoption (Harvey AI, CoCounsel) but are priced out of enterprise licenses ($100k+ minimums). Existing consumer AI tools lack the **audit trails**, **privileged data silos**, and **citation rigor** required for legal work.

## 2. Target Audience
- Boutique litigation firms.
- Family offices.
- Specialized regulatory practices.

## 3. Core Product Features (The 'Sovereign Firm' Package)

### A. The Orchestration Layer
- **Conflict of Interest Gate:** An agent that scans every prompt against a local database of clients/adverse parties before processing.
- **Multi-Agent Review Quorum:** Every legal memorandum is drafted by one agent and critiqued by two independent "senior counsel" agents before being presented to the human attorney.

### B. The Audit Trail Engine
- **Verbatim Citations:** The swarm is hard-coded to only use provided case files or verified legal databases (via MCP tools). No hallucinations allowed.
- **Decision Logic Logs:** Every output includes a hidden "Thinking Trace" (like the one I use) that shows exactly which documents were referenced and why certain conclusions were drawn.

### C. Secure Local Ingestion
- Files are processed on the firm's own hardware (Mac Studio or secure cloud tenant) to maintain attorney-client privilege and data sovereignty.

## 4. Competitive Moat
- **Low Slop Ratio:** Unlike generic LLM apps, SimplifiedClaw is tuned for "Low-Entropy" output.
- **Price Advantage:** $499/user/month with a $15,000 "Institutional Setup" fee (Disrupting the $1k/mo Harvey price point).

## 5. Development Roadmap (24-Hour Prototypes)
- **Tool 1:** A "Case File Chronology Agent" that turns 500 PDFs into a surgical timeline of events.
- **Tool 2:** A "Deposition Prep Swarm" that identifies inconsistencies in witness testimony across multiple documents.
