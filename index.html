What anchors the work

Accessibility-oriented products, practical publishing, and supporting infrastructure that hold up in the real world.

Anchovy Labs builds products; we do not sell consulting, agency, or professional services.

Accessibility-oriented consumer software

We focus on products that help people navigate the parts of life where software is unclear, brittle, or needlessly hard to use.

AI-native product design

We use AI where it creates real leverage: clearer interfaces, better assistance, and products that help users act instead of getting stuck.

Publishing as product

Anchovy Labs Press extends the same mission in written form: practical material that helps people regain clarity and agency inside confusing systems.

Durable internal agentic infrastructure

Behind the products is a serious internal systems layer for research, iteration, publishing, and operations. Infrastructure matters because reliability matters.

Science & AI as an enabling layer

Our AI thesis is grounded, product-oriented, and infrastructure-heavy: retrieval over drift, orchestration over theater, and systems that make repeated software delivery more reliable across products, publishing, and operations.

Why this company exists

  • We build products that expand agency, not software that adds more noise.
  • Accessibility is not a feature pass. It is a product strategy.
  • AI is useful when it makes action clearer, faster, and more dignified for the person using it.
  • We would rather ship a durable product than a clever experiment that distorts what the company is.
