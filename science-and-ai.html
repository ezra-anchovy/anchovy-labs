Our mission

We build technology that expands human agency. In practice, that means products that help people move through communication, daily structure, decision-making, and digital systems with more clarity, capability, and dignity — especially when those systems are otherwise confusing, inaccessible, or fragile.

This is why our product work spans accessibility-oriented apps, AI-native tools, and publishing through Anchovy Labs Press. The common thread is not category. It is making complex systems easier for ordinary people to use well.

Our AI thesis

We are not interested in AI as theater. We care about AI when it makes products more grounded, more useful, and more repeatable.

Grounded search over guesswork

Useful AI systems need reliable access to curated knowledge, not just plausible generation. We prefer grounded retrieval and explicit evidence over free-floating synthesis.

Durable state over context loss

Long-running product work breaks when systems lose memory at exactly the wrong moment. State permanence, checkpointing, and survivable workflows are architectural unlocks for us.

Workflow orchestration over one-shot prompting

Real products come from loops: review, verify, reconcile, ship. We build orchestration and verification layers so AI contributes to mature product delivery rather than isolated demos.

Accessibility as a systems problem

Accessibility is not only UI polish. It is about reducing ambiguity, cognitive load, and failure points across the whole user experience.

Why AI infrastructure matters

We care about AI infrastructure because product quality depends on it. Grounded retrieval, durable state, and workflow orchestration are not backend luxuries for us — they are the difference between brittle demos and systems that can support research, publishing, and software delivery over time.

Platforms like Vertex matter because they unlock capabilities we actually use: grounded search over curated knowledge, repeatable orchestration patterns, and a more durable architectural spine for long-running product workflows.

We are not looking for infrastructure to power generic model experimentation. We are building a repeatable product organization whose internal systems need grounded retrieval, continuity, and orchestration to work well.
