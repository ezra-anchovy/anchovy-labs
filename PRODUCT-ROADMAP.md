# Anchovy Labs Product Roadmap

## Current Portfolio (Q1 2026)

### SimplifiedClaw — Enterprise AI Agent Deployment
- **Target**: Mid-market and enterprise teams
- **Pricing**: $500 - Custom (+ monthly support)
- **Core Value**: Expert deployment, security, ongoing support

### UrClaw — 1-on-1 Developer Setup
- **Target**: Individual developers, enthusiasts
- **Pricing**: $99 - $499 (one-time)
- **Core Value**: Skip the docs, get running fast

---

## Near-Term Opportunities (Q2-Q3 2026)

### 1. ClawWatch — Agent Monitoring SaaS
**Problem**: Once agents are deployed, teams need visibility into what they're doing.

**Features**:
- Real-time agent activity dashboard
- Cost tracking per agent/task
- Anomaly detection (unusual API usage, cost spikes)
- Compliance logging for regulated industries

**Pricing Model**: $49/mo starter, $199/mo team, custom enterprise

**Why Now**: Natural upsell from SimplifiedClaw clients. Recurring revenue.

---

### 2. SkillVault — Curated Skill Marketplace
**Problem**: The skill ecosystem is fragmented. Security concerns abound (see recent malware reports).

**Features**:
- Security-vetted skills only
- One-click install
- Enterprise license management
- Private skill hosting for teams

**Pricing Model**: 30% revenue share on paid skills, $99/mo for enterprise private hosting

**Why Now**: ClawHub exists but lacks enterprise trust layer.

---

### 3. ClawCert — Training & Certification
**Problem**: Teams need to upskill on agentic AI. No formal training exists.

**Features**:
- Self-paced video courses
- Hands-on labs
- Certification exams
- Corporate training packages

**Pricing Model**: $299 individual cert, $2K+ corporate packages

**Why Now**: First-mover advantage in OpenClaw education.

---

## Medium-Term Opportunities (Q4 2026+)

### 4. ClawHost — Managed Agent Hosting
**Problem**: Some clients want fully managed infrastructure.

**Features**:
- Pre-configured VMs with OpenClaw
- Automatic updates and security patches
- 24/7 monitoring
- Isolated environments per client

**Pricing Model**: $199-999/mo depending on compute

**Why Now**: Higher margin than consulting, recurring revenue.

---

### 5. ClawStudio — White-Label Agent Platform
**Problem**: Agencies and consultancies want to offer AI agents under their own brand.

**Features**:
- White-label dashboard
- Custom branding
- Client management
- Revenue share or licensing

**Pricing Model**: $5K+ setup, $500/mo platform fee

**Why Now**: B2B2B model, high LTV.

---

## Product Principles

1. **Each product is a separate brand** — Clear positioning, dedicated landing page
2. **Recurring revenue preferred** — Consulting is lumpy; SaaS is predictable
3. **Build on consulting insights** — Every client engagement surfaces product opportunities
4. **Security is a feature** — Every product emphasizes safe deployment
5. **Start with MVP, iterate fast** — Ship in weeks, not months
