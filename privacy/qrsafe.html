QRion Scanner Privacy Policy

QRion Scanner uses the camera solely for QR code scanning. No camera data is stored or transmitted.

Camera Use

The camera is used exclusively to scan QR codes. Video and image data from the camera are processed in real-time and never stored or transmitted to any server.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Scanned QR code content remains on your device.
